## Overview
AI avatar platform that trains a digital clone from video footage. Records a person speaking → trains a personalized AI avatar → avatar can speak any script with lip-synced output. Used by the 100x/AVTV team to produce content without being on camera.

## Key Contributions / Role
- Avatar training: 2–5 min recording → Avatar 3 (lip sync fix) or Avatar 5 (full quality)
- Takes uploaded ElevenLabs audio — does not require its own voice (use upload, not HeyGen's TTS)
- AI Studio: timeline editor, B-roll support, media library (used by Sridev)
- Avatar Shorts: short-form video generation (similar to CDance)
- Human editor still required for final output (adds B-rolls, effects in After Effects + Premiere)
