## Overview

AEOS Labs is an AI consulting and Forward Deployed Engineering firm used as a case study in 100x Cohort 7. Core team includes Tejas (CTO/engineering), Varun (marketing domain expertise), and Pranay. The firm exemplifies the FDE model — domain expertise combined with AI system design.

## Key Contributions / Role

Secured high-profile enterprise contracts by leading with marketing domain expertise, not generic AI capability:

- **Amazon Prime**: MedZap S3 campaign
- **Netflix**: AI content/marketing system work
- **Ogilvy**: AI-assisted advertising copy review system (the Ogilvy copy-reviewer prompt is referenced as an eval design example in 100x)

Why they won: Amazon Prime and similar clients did not choose TCS or Accenture (large IT vendors). They chose AEOS because Varun and team had genuine marketing domain expertise — they could define "what great marketing output looks like" and encode it as an eval system. That domain depth is not replicable by general AI engineers.
