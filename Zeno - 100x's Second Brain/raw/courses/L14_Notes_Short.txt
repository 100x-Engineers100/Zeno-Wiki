ELEVENLABS

Voice clone types:
- Voice Design: describe vocal characteristics in text → generates a new unique voice
- Instant Clone: upload short audio, quick clone
- Professional Clone: upload 2+ hours of varied speech → most accurate, near-identical

Creating speech with clone:
- Model: Multilingual V2 (most used by 100x team). 11V3 is more expressive but less preferred.
- Speed: reduce from fast for natural pace
- Stability: 50% default. Higher = more variability in unfamiliar word pronunciation.
- Similarity: 70–75%. 100% breaks output.
- Style Exaggeration: 35–40. Higher = over-dramatization.

Multilingual output: paste any language text → model speaks it in your cloned voice. Works for Spanish, Russian, Japanese, etc.

Voice Changer: upload any video → replaces audio with your cloned voice. Doesn't change accent, just voice texture. Used by creators adding their voice to AI-generated Kling/CDance clips.

Sound Effects: prompt + duration → generates 4 variations. ~10 credits/second.
Music: 11 Labs charges ~1500–1600 credits/min. Not great. Use Suno instead.
Flows: node-based workflow builder, similar to FreePik Spaces.
11 Agents: LLM-based, covered in next module.

Open source voice alternatives: FITTS, Chatterbox, Index TTS.

Custom voice without copyright issues: use Voice Design, describe characteristics (don't name the celebrity), generate a new voice with those traits.

HEYGEN

What it does: records you → trains an AI avatar → avatar can speak any script.

Avatar training requirements:
- Record 2–5 minutes of yourself speaking to camera
- Maintain constant eye contact, no hands in front of face
- Upload via Avatars → Clone a Real Person → Upload → Create Avatar

Avatar model versions:
- Avatar 3: fixes lip sync only, preserves original footage expression/hands. Best when training footage isn't perfect.
- Avatar 4: downgrade.
- Avatar 5: better overall but can distort expressions if training wasn't clean.

Full pipeline (what the 100x team actually does):
1. Write script
2. Generate audio in ElevenLabs with cloned voice → download file
3. HeyGen → your avatar → Add Script → Upload (the downloaded audio)
4. Don't select a voice in HeyGen — it uses your uploaded audio as-is
5. Select Avatar 3 or 5, set resolution 1080, landscape/portrait
6. Submit → download output → hand to human editor

AI Studio in HeyGen: adds timeline editor, B-roll support, media library. Sridev uses this.

Human editor is still required. AI generates the A-roll; editor adds B-rolls, cuts, effects in After Effects + Premiere Pro.

Avatar Shorts: HeyGen's short-form video feature, similar to CDance. New.
